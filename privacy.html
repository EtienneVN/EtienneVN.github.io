Privacy policy - GameJournal rclone app

GameJournal rclone app is a personal tool used only by its owner, Etienne Van Niekerk.

It accesses the owner's own Google Drive solely to upload and download the owner's own files. It does not collect, store, sell or share any data from anyone else, and no data is sent to any server other than Google Drive.

Access can be revoked at any time at https://myaccount.google.com/permissions.
